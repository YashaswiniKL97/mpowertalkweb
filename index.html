THE VOICE BEHIND MPOWER TALK

Kishore Rachaiah
Founder, mPOWER TALK

Every idea begins somewhere.

For Kishore, it began with a belief that meaningful conversations can change the way people think, connect and move forward.

That belief became mPOWER TALK — a space created around people, ideas and conversations.

Every conversation begins with a voice.

Every voice has a story.

Every person has something worth sharing.

Ideas become conversations.

A simple idea can start a meaningful conversation.

We listen. We connect.

Different voices bring different perspectives.

Conversations create change.

Conversations can turn ideas into action.
